Conclusion / Key Insights

GIS Lab 2026 Core Quantitative Summary

The land cover-pollution correlation analysis across Turkey (2021–2023) has yielded three core indicators of environmental exposure. Forest (Tree Cover) boundaries proved to mitigate particulates up to 14%, while crops witnessed high PM₂.₅ concentrations post-harvest. Bivariate grid overlay serves as an exceptional tool identifying critical pollution hotspots in Turkish metropolitan areas.
